# Google's Local Service Ads vs. Traditional PPC for Law Firms: The Numbers Don't Lie

Should your law firm use Local Service Ads? Yes — and if you're not already running them, you're probably handing leads to the firm down the street. How do Local Service Ads compare to Google Ads for law firms? They operate on completely different logic: traditional PPC charges you for clicks whether or not that click turns into a conversation, while LSAs charge you only when a verified lead contacts you directly. For **Local Service Ads law firms Florida** markets, that distinction is not a minor footnote. It's the whole ballgame.

Here's what most managing partners don't know: a significant portion of law firms eligible for LSAs in Florida aren't running them. Not because they made a strategic decision to skip it. Because nobody told them they qualified.

---

## What Local Service Ads Actually Are (And Why They're Not Google Ads)

**Local Service Ads** (LSAs) appear above everything else in a Google search — above the traditional PPC ads, above the map pack, above organic results. They show your firm's name, your Google rating, your general location, and a phone number or message button. The "Google Screened" or "Google Guaranteed" badge sits right next to your name.

That badge matters. It signals to a prospective client that Google has vetted your firm — background checks, bar verification, licensing confirmation. For a potential client who just experienced a car accident, received divorce papers, or is staring down a criminal charge, that badge does real credibility work before they've read a single word of your website copy.

Traditional Google Ads work differently. You bid on keywords, write ad copy, set targeting parameters, and pay every time someone clicks your ad — regardless of whether they call you, fill out a form, or bounce immediately because they were actually looking for legal *forms*, not legal *representation*. The keyword-to-click-to-lead pipeline leaks at every joint.

LSAs skip most of that. You pay per lead, not per click.

---

## The Cost-Per-Lead Reality for Florida Law Firms

Let's be honest about what traditional PPC looks like in competitive Florida legal markets — personal injury in Tampa, family law in Orlando, criminal defense in Miami. **Click costs** in those practice areas routinely run into the dozens or low hundreds of dollars per click. Legal is consistently one of the most expensive keyword categories in Google's entire ecosystem.

Now do the math on conversion rates. If a well-optimized landing page converts at 5% — which would be considered solid performance — you're paying for 20 clicks to get one contact form submission. Multiply that by your average click cost and you'll understand why your [client acquisition cost](https://hugheyllc.com/blog/client-acquisition-cost-threshold-law-firms/) keeps creeping upward even when campaign performance looks stable on paper.

LSAs restructure the equation. You're not paying for 20 clicks hoping one converts. You're paying when someone actually calls or messages your firm through the ad. Your cost-per-lead is your cost-per-lead, not a derivative of click cost and conversion rate guesswork.

This doesn't mean LSAs are cheap — competitive practice areas still carry meaningful per-lead costs. But **the pricing model is transparent and directly tied to actual contacts**, not traffic volume. That's a fundamentally different risk profile.

If you've been wrestling with the [lead quality vs. lead volume](https://hugheyllc.com/blog/lead-quality-vs-volume-law-firms/) problem, LSAs at least tighten the top of the funnel by ensuring you're only paying for people who took the step of contacting you.

## Who Qualifies for LSAs in Florida

Google has steadily expanded LSA eligibility for legal practice areas. As of now, eligible categories include personal injury, family law, criminal defense, estate law, real estate law, bankruptcy, immigration, and several others — though eligibility continues to evolve and varies by market.

The verification process requires:
- Proof of active bar membership
- Background checks on firm owners and staff who interact with clients
- Malpractice insurance verification
- A Google Business Profile in good standing

This is not a two-hour setup. The verification process takes time, and if your Google Business Profile has issues — inconsistent NAP data, unclaimed listings, suppressed duplicates — those problems will surface and slow everything down. Sort those out before you apply.

For **Local Service Ads law firms Florida** eligibility specifically, Google's geographic coverage across major Florida metros is solid. Smaller markets may have lighter competition, which can actually work in your favor on cost.

## Where Traditional PPC Still Has the Edge

LSAs are not a complete replacement for traditional paid search, and anyone telling you otherwise is oversimplifying.

**Traditional PPC advantages:**
- Full control over ad copy, landing page destination, and messaging
- Ability to target specific keywords, including long-tail and practice-specific terms
- Remarketing capabilities — LSAs don't remarket
- Useful for practice areas not yet covered by LSA eligibility
- Better for testing messaging and offers

If you're running a boutique firm with a narrow specialty, traditional PPC paired with a tightly focused landing page can outperform a broader LSA approach — especially if you've already done the work of aligning your [keyword intent](https://hugheyllc.com/blog/keyword-intent-mismatch-law-firms/) with what your ideal client is actually searching for.

The smarter play for most Florida law firms is **running both simultaneously**. LSAs capture the top-of-page visibility and the verification-badge trust signal. Traditional PPC captures the specific, intent-rich searches where you control the message and destination.

What you're trying to avoid is treating them as the same thing and measuring them the same way. They're not.

## What Most Firms Get Wrong About LSA Management

Running LSAs is not the same as setting them up and forgetting them. The things that kill LSA performance:

**Ignoring disputes.** Every lead you receive through LSA is disputable if it doesn't meet Google's criteria — wrong practice area, no intent to hire, solicitors, etc. Most firms don't dispute enough. That's money left on the table.

**Letting reviews stagnate.** Your LSA ranking is heavily influenced by your review count, recency, and rating. A firm with 200 reviews will outrank a firm with 20 in the same market at the same bid level. Your [social proof strategy](https://hugheyllc.com/blog/law-firm-social-proof-strategy/) directly affects your LSA visibility.

**Setting bids and walking away.** LSA budgets and bid strategies need active management, especially in competitive markets where other firms are adjusting aggressively.

**Not connecting LSAs to intake tracking.** If you can't trace an LSA lead through your intake process to a signed client, you can't calculate your true ROI. You're just guessing.
